The farm

A studio built into the hillside

Shirebrook Studio is the photography arm of our family farm. Our round-doored studio is set right into the hillside — step out of the portrait chair and you're standing in a meadow, not a hallway.

We shoot almost entirely with natural light: open shade under the party tree, golden hour across the east pasture, and a warm barn studio for rainy days. Kids relax faster out here, and it shows in the photos.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What should students wear?

Solid colors photograph best on green backdrops — navy, burgundy, cream, and sage all work beautifully. Avoid logos and neon green; it competes with the grass.

What happens if it rains?

Picture day moves into the red barn studio, which has beautiful big-window light. We monitor the forecast and make the call by 6 a.m. on picture day.

How fast do families get proofs?

Private online proof galleries go out within 7 days of picture day. Rush turnaround is available for yearbook deadlines.

Do you travel to our school?

We shoot everything at the farm — that's the whole point! We do set up a temporary indoor studio at partner schools for make-up days only.

Can we book a farm visit for a field trip?

Yes — we host a limited number of class farm visits each season alongside portrait sessions. Ask when you book.
